## a. October 2026 CPI release: **Thu 12 Nov 2026, 4:00 PM IST**: PASS

- **Date.** MoSPI's own September 2026 CPI press release (published 12 Oct 2026) says: "CPI for October 2026 will be
  released on 12th November, 2026 (Thursday) or the next working day" if the 12th is a holiday.
  [MoSPI press release, Sep 2026](https://www.mospi.gov.in/uploads/latestReleases/latest_release_1791800601071_643518af-5320-4e52-b78c-75c92b83885b_Press_Release_for_the_month_of_September,_2026.pdf).
  The Advance Release Calendar 2026-27 also lists "12th Nov: All India Consumer Price Index (CPI)":
  [ARC 2026-27 (updated 25 May 2026)](https://www.mospi.gov.in/uploads/documents/releaseCalender/1779709510470-ADVANCE%20RELEASE%20CALENDAR%202026-27%20Updated%2025.05.2026.pdf).
- **Time.** 4:00 PM IST. The PIB posting of the August 2026 CPI is time-stamped 14 Sep 2026, 4:00 PM:
  [PIB, CPI Aug 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2310058&lang=2&reg=48).
  The June 2025 release was "embargoed till 4:00 PM of 14th July, 2025". One unofficial explainer still says 5:30 PM. That was
  the old practice, so ignore it.
- 12 Nov 2026 is a Thursday. No source says it is a holiday.

## b. November 2026 CPI (listed for Sat 12 Dec 2026): most likely **Mon 14 Dec 2026, 4:00 PM IST**

MoSPI's standing rule is "the 12th, or the next working day if the 12th is a holiday". Each time the 12th fell on a Saturday,
MoSPI released on the **following Monday**:

| 12th was a Saturday | Actually released | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 12 Oct 2024 (Sep 2024 CPI) | Mon 14 Oct 2024 | [PIB release, 14 Oct 2024](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2024/oct/doc20241014416101.pdf) |
| 12 Jul 2025 (Jun 2025 CPI) | Mon 14 Jul 2025, 4 PM | [All India Radio, 14 Jul 2025](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/indias-retail-inflation-falls-to-2-10-in-june-lowest-since-2019) and the MoSPI embargo note |
| 12 Sep 2026 (Aug 2026 CPI, **this year, new 2024 series**) | Mon 14 Sep 2026, 4 PM | [PIB, CPI Aug 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2310058&lang=2&reg=48). The July release had announced "14th September, 2026 (Monday)" in advance. |

So the most likely date is **Mon 14 Dec 2026, 4:00 PM IST**. That is inside judging, which ends 15 Dec 12:00 PM PT (= 16 Dec 01:30 IST).
**Not yet officially confirmed.** The search did not show the ARC's December line. MoSPI normally announces the next date
inside each press release, so check the last paragraph of the 12 Nov release. Unclear: whether 14 Dec is a holiday
(nothing found suggesting it is).
