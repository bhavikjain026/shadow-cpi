## What's uncertain

- The 80% range comes from past misses. In the replay the official figure fell inside it in only 5 of 9 months, so treat it as narrower than its label.
- 79% of the basket (most services, rent, clothing) is carried on trend, not measured.
- The counter-search for evidence pointing the other way found 0 verified contrary quotes.
- Milk price announcements are recorded but not applied (milk is measured through the government spine); an audit removed a Mumbai wholesale milk rate from this month (docs/after_scoring_changes.md).

## How it turned out (hindsight, replay only)
