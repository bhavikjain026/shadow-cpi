# Pre-registration: outside comparator for the live months (October and November 2026)

Written and committed on 2026-10-27 (14:12 IST), **before 12 Nov 2026**, so before any live month's official
figure or outside forecast for it has been seen. It fixes which outside forecast our sealed nowcasts are compared
with. It changes no model, range or sealed number.

## The comparator
1. **Primary: the Reuters economists' poll.** The median of the last Reuters poll of economists for that month's
   all-India CPI inflation (year on year), in an article publicly timestamped before MoSPI's 4 pm IST release.
2. **Backup: CNBC-TV18.** Used only when there is no qualifying Reuters poll: CNBC-TV18's headline consensus for
   that month, explicitly labelled as a consensus or poll, timestamped before 4 pm IST on release day.
3. **Otherwise: "missing".** No other source fills the gap, and the comparison for that month is reported as
   missing, not dropped.

Not separate forecasts:
- Reuters copies on other sites (syndicated or rewritten poll stories) are the same poll, not a second forecast;
  they can only help find the Reuters article.
- RBI quarterly projections are context only, never the comparator.

## What is saved, before 4 pm IST on release day
For the chosen article: its URL, its public timestamp, the exact line that states the median (or consensus), and
the SHA-256 of the saved copy. An article saved at or after 4 pm IST does not qualify.
