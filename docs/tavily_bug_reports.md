# Tavily feedback

Generated by `python tools/build_bug_reports.py` from committed results files (counts are read, not typed). The top 3 lead; each has what we observed, what we expected, a suggested fix, and evidence (counts and Tavily request IDs, so the calls can be looked up).

## Top 3

### 1. auto_parameters picks a recent time_range for queries about a past month

- **What we did:** 72 searches with auto_parameters: true, a query naming a month in Jan–Sep 2026, and max_results only (no dates).
- **Observed:** auto_parameters chose time_range w 57, d 14, m 1; 620 of 659 results were dated October 2026 and 38 were undated.
- **Expected:** a time_range that covers the month the query names, or none, with a note that a historical query needs start_date/end_date.
- **Suggested fix:** when the query names a past month, return no time_range (or that month's window); document the default.
- **Evidence:** 620 of 659 results from October 2026, 72 searches; Tavily request IDs logged for 31 of 72 searches, e.g. `73a3cac7-ae69-4e16-847d-726c5baec6c3`, `22b874a7-56ab-4d36-b4f6-73c737b18e62`; files `v2/ablation/arm_T_*.json` (plan[].auto_parameters, cards[].published).

### 2. With explicit dates, auto_parameters still reports a time_range

- **What we did:** 56 searches with auto_parameters: true plus our start_date/end_date (arm T+dates, exploratory).
- **Observed:** the auto_parameters echo in the response reported time_range w 47, d 8, y 1, and a start_date or end_date in 0 of 56 echoes; yet 532 of 532 dated results fell inside our window, so the dates were applied.
- **Expected:** the echo shows the window actually applied (our start_date/end_date).
- **Suggested fix:** echo start_date/end_date, or drop time_range from the echo, when the caller sends dates.
- **Evidence:** 532 of 532 dated results inside the window, 56 searches; Tavily request IDs logged for 47 of 56 searches, e.g. `0e8e93b5-57d6-49a7-b1bf-204b2c6f4249`, `e63b56ba-9a8c-407a-acbc-215c66d4dcce`; files `v2/ablation/arm_Td_*.json` (plan[].auto_parameters, plan[].dates_kept).

### 3. Extract has no as-of view, so Search dates do not freeze page content

- **What we did:** 14 finance-check searches with start_date/end_date and filter_by_published_date, then a separate, post-hoc AI-assisted review (ChatGPT agent) of every result page (146 of 283 rows stayed unclear).
- **Observed:** 0 of 14 searches returned a result dated after the cutoff, yet 1 page's current version leaks the outcome: R196 was dated 2026-07-07 by Tavily (cutoff 2026-07-08) and updated on 2026-07-09 with a link to the actual result.
- **Expected:** a way for a time-locked user to read, or at least detect, the page as it was on the cutoff.
- **Suggested fix:** an as-of / crawl-date field on Extract (the date of the copy returned), or an as_of parameter.
- **Evidence:** 1 of 67 filtered result pages; R196 from the search with request ID `dce7e851-b012-4dfe-84d4-ea43b27b1bd9` (response SHA-256 `a509b8bc8a346701…`); file `results/timelock34_second_review.json` (finance_filtered.example).

## Feature requests

### 4. Separate original-publication and last-modified dates

- **What we did:** compared, by code, Tavily's published_date with the page's own published/updated date on the 42 distinct date-test pages that had both.
- **Observed:** 4 had a later published_date (3 by more than 7 days; largest gap 345 days).
- **Expected:** nothing different: this is consistent with Tavily's documented definition of published_date (published or last updated, beta).
- **Suggested fix:** return original-publication and last-modified dates as two fields, and let the date filter choose.
- **Evidence:** 4 of 42 pages; file `results/date_field_test.json` (rows[].arms[].results[].tavily_date, page_date).

## Smaller observations

### 5. Map returns empty results silently

- **What we did:** Map on the 5 sites of our official crawl list and 7 dairy sites, with and without instructions.
- **Observed:** empty results with no failed_results and no error on 5 sites of the official crawl list (merc, derc, uperc, iocl, amul) and 3 dairy sites (aavin, nandini-kmf, sanchi-indore (MPCDF union)); without instructions also empty on merc, derc, uperc, iocl; a bad start URL did get a clear error (sanchi-mpcdf).
- **Expected:** an empty answer that says why (blocked, robots, nothing matched).
- **Suggested fix:** a reason field on empty Map/Crawl responses.
- **Evidence:** files `results/prompt_t/official_crawl_2026-10.json`, `dairy_map_2026-10-21.json`, `map_noinstr.json`.

### 6. One scanned PDF: Extract returned the letterhead but not the image-only rates

- **What we did:** Extract (basic and advanced) on one scanned dairy rate-list PDF (Gokul).
- **Observed:** 607 characters of letterhead; the rates are an image and did not come back.
- **Expected:** OCR of image-only PDF pages, or a flag that the text layer is empty.
- **Suggested fix:** an ocr option, or an 'image_only' flag on the result.
- **Evidence:** file `results/prompt_t/dairy_followup_2026-10-21.json` (gokul_adv_chars).

### 7. /usage lags per-call usage

- **What we did:** compared GET /usage with the per-call include_usage credits the same day.
- **Observed:** /usage reported plan_usage 6 while per-call include_usage credits added up to 226.
- **Expected:** /usage close to the sum of per-call usage.
- **Suggested fix:** update /usage within minutes, or document the lag.
- **Evidence:** file `results/feedback_evidence/tavily_usage_2026-10-20.json`.
