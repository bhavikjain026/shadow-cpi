# Feedback as bug reports

Generated by `python tools/build_bug_reports.py` from committed results files (counts are read, not typed).

## 1. auto_parameters picked a recent time_range when no dates were sent (observed in our test) (Tavily)

- **What we did:** 72 searches with auto_parameters: true, a query and max_results only, for months Jan–Sep 2026.
- **What happened:** time_range w 57, d 14, m 1; 620 of 659 results dated October 2026, 38 undated.
- **What we expected:** a documented default, or a warning that a historical query needs start_date/end_date.
- **Suggested fix:** document the behaviour; let auto_parameters return no time_range for queries naming a past month.
- **Evidence:** `v2/ablation/arm_T_*.json (plan[].auto_parameters, cards[].published)`.

## 2. auto_parameters reports a time_range that explicit dates override (Tavily)

- **What we did:** 56 searches with auto_parameters plus start_date/end_date.
- **What happened:** auto_parameters echoed time_range w 47, d 8, y 1, yet 532 of 532 dated results fell inside the window.
- **What we expected:** the echo shows the window actually applied.
- **Suggested fix:** echo start_date/end_date (or drop time_range) when the caller sends dates.
- **Evidence:** `v2/ablation/arm_Td_*.json (plan[].auto_parameters, plan[].dates_kept)`.

## 3. published_date later than the page's own date (consistent with the documented definition) (Tavily)

- **What we did:** compared, by code (not a hand check), Tavily's published_date with the page's own published/updated date on the 42 distinct date-test pages that had both (sample: results/date_field_test.json).
- **What happened:** 4 had a later published_date (3 by more than 7 days; largest gap 345 days).
- **What we expected:** nothing different: this is consistent with Tavily's documented definition of published_date (published or last updated, beta).
- **Suggested fix:** suggestion: separate original-publication and last-modified fields.
- **Evidence:** `results/date_field_test.json (rows[].arms[].results[].tavily_date, page_date)`.

## 4. Map returns 0 results silently (Tavily)

- **What we did:** Map on the 5 sites of our official crawl list and 7 dairy sites, with and without instructions.
- **What happened:** empty results with no failed_results and no error on 5 sites of the official crawl list (merc, derc, uperc, iocl, amul) and 3 dairy sites (aavin, nandini-kmf, sanchi-indore (MPCDF union)); without instructions also empty on merc, derc, uperc, iocl; a bad start URL did get a clear error (sanchi-mpcdf).
- **What we expected:** an empty answer that says why (blocked, robots, nothing matched).
- **Suggested fix:** a reason field on empty Map/Crawl responses.
- **Evidence:** `results/prompt_t/official_crawl_2026-10.json, dairy_map_2026-10-21.json, map_noinstr.json`.

## 5. Scanned PDFs return only the letterhead (Tavily)

- **What we did:** Extract (basic and advanced) on Gokul's dairy rate-list PDF.
- **What happened:** 607 characters of letterhead; the rates are an image.
- **What we expected:** OCR of image-only PDF pages, or a flag that the text layer is empty.
- **Suggested fix:** an ocr option, or an 'image_only' flag on the result.
- **Evidence:** `results/prompt_t/dairy_followup_2026-10-21.json (gokul_adv_chars)`.

## 6. /usage lags per-call usage (Tavily)

- **What we did:** compared GET /usage with the per-call include_usage credits the same day.
- **What happened:** /usage reported plan_usage 6 while per-call include_usage credits added up to 226.
- **What we expected:** /usage close to the sum of per-call usage.
- **Suggested fix:** update /usage within minutes, or document the lag.
- **Evidence:** `results/feedback_evidence/tavily_usage_2026-10-20.json`.

## 7. Nano gives no number where Super finds one on the same page (Nebius / NVIDIA (Nemotron Nano))

- **What we did:** Nano read each fetched page; where it gave no number, Super re-read the page (63 saved arm and evidence files).
- **What happened:** Super re-read 1692 pages and found a verified number (the number is in the cited line) on 43 of them.
- **What we expected:** a number whenever the cited line states one.
- **Suggested fix:** a schema-level 'required number when a currency or % is present' hint, or a confidence field.
- **Evidence:** `v2/ablation/arm_*.json, v2/evidence/events_*.json (cards[].super_reread, claims[].read_by)`.

## 8. Extract reads the page as it is today (suggestion) (Tavily)

- **What we did:** 14 finance-check searches with start_date/end_date and filter_by_published_date, then an independent AI-assisted second review of every result page (post-hoc).
- **What happened:** 0 of 14 searches returned a result dated after the cutoff, yet 1 page's current version leaks the outcome: R196 was dated 2026-07-07 (cutoff 2026-07-08) and updated on 2026-07-09.
- **What we expected:** a way for a time-locked user to tell that the page Extract returns changed after the cutoff.
- **Suggested fix:** an as-of / crawl-date field for Extract, so time-locked users can tell if a page changed.
- **Evidence:** `results/timelock34_second_review.json (finance_filtered.example)`.
